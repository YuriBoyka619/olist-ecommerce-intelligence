# Power BI Report

## Olist E-commerce Analytics

Interactive Power BI report built on the `sm_olist_ecommerce_gold` semantic model.

### Report Pages

1. Executive Overview
2. Sales & Order Analysis
3. Delivery & Customer Experience
4. Customer Reviews & Experience
5. ML Risk Intelligence

### Key Analytics

- Order and revenue KPIs
- Product and seller performance
- Delivery performance and late-delivery analysis
- Customer review sentiment and experience
- Machine-learning late-delivery risk predictions

### ML Risk Intelligence

The report integrates predictions from the registered late-delivery ML model.

Key metrics include:
- Scored Orders: 12,507
- Predicted Late Orders: 3,609
- High Risk Orders: 726
- Average Late Probability: 6.92%
